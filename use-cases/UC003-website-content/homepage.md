## What BIMpact is

BIMpact is a Context Information Model and an Intent Management System.

The source of truth is structured context. Documents, specs, dashboards, and tools are derived views.

---

## Why this exists

BIMpact started in BIM practice and evolved through real delivery constraints.

The founder journey:

- architect
- BIM coordinator
- BIM management
- BIM-exclusive service delivery
- expansion into data science, AI, quality management, and process automation

The key shift was applying BIM logic to AI and delivery context:

- centralized single source of truth
- collaborative model with explicit ownership
- related and traceable data entities
- versioned change history
