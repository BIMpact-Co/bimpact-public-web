# BIMpact.io - Public Administration Domain

Maturity: emerging

## Positioning

Public administration work requires strong process clarity, governance discipline, and operational traceability.

This domain adapts BIMpact method to high-accountability environments.

---

## Typical scope

- process mapping for multi-actor service delivery
- requirement and decision structuring for policy-constrained workflows
- compliance-aware transformation of legacy process chains
- audit-oriented documentation and validation readiness

---

## Why this fits BIMpact

The context model approach reduces ambiguity where decisions, rules, and responsibilities are distributed across teams and institutions.

---

## Boundary

This page presents public administration as a domain specialization.

It remains secondary to core platform identity and does not replace sector-specific legal advisory work.
