# BIMpact.io - BI and Analytics Domain

Maturity: emerging

## Positioning

This domain focuses on turning fragmented business data into decision-ready, traceable analytical outputs.

The method is context-first: data models, definitions, and decisions are explicit before dashboarding.

---

## Typical scope

- Power BI semantic model design and governance
- KPI dictionary alignment and metric traceability
- Excel-integrated operational workflows where lightweight collaboration is needed
- data quality and refresh reliability checks

---

## Why this fits BIMpact

The same logic used in BIM and system delivery applies here:

- single source of truth over scattered spreadsheets
- linked entities over isolated reports
- explicit decision and requirement chain for metric design

---

## Boundary

This page describes BI specialization capability.

It does not redefine BIMpact as a BI-only service.
