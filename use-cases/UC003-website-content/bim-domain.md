# BIMpact.io - BIM Domain

## Why a dedicated BIM page

BIMpact began as a BIM-exclusive company and retains deep BIM execution capability.

This page isolates BIM specialization so the core platform identity remains context-first and domain-agnostic.

The BIM specialization comes from direct field progression:

This practical chain informs current engineering choices, quality controls, and user adoption strategy.
