# BIMpact.io - Maker Lab (Arduino / Raspberry)

Maturity: exploratory

## Positioning

This is an exploration track for AI-assisted physical prototyping workflows.

The idea: describe a product concept and generate a first-pass kit proposal (components, cost estimate, and starter code).

---

## Exploration themes

- parts suggestion from functional intent
- bill-of-materials drafts with cost approximation ranges
- starter firmware/code templates for Arduino or Raspberry Pi
- iteration loop between prototype behavior and requirement refinement

---

## Why this is interesting

It extends BIMpact context logic beyond software-only delivery:

- intent first
- structured decomposition
- traceable derivations from needs to implementation artefacts

---

## Boundary and caution

This page is intentionally exploratory.

No production delivery promise is made until at least one validated case exists with repeatable quality and safety criteria.
