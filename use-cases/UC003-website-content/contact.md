# BIMpact.io - Contact

## Start with a discovery call

If your team has delivery friction between requirements and implementation, BIMpact can help structure the chain and reduce ambiguity.

Typical fit:

- fragmented requirements and unclear ownership
- repeated rework caused by hidden decisions
- delivery handoffs that lose context

---

## What to include in your first message

1. your current process in one paragraph
2. the highest-cost bottleneck you want to remove
3. whether you need analysis-only or end-to-end delivery
4. your target timeline
