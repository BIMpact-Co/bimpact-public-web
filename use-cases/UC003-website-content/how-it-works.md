# BIMpact.io - How It Works

## Core principle

Structured context is the source of truth.

Deliverables are generated views that remain linked to their origin, rationale, and validation path.

---

## Lifecycle flow

![Lifecycle Pipeline](diagrams/lifecycle-pipeline.svg)

1. capture context and constraints
2. perform functional analysis
3. ratify architecture decisions
4. generate machine-readable specifications
5. implement with explicit boundaries
6. prepare deployment and operations
7. capture feedback and feed the next cycle

---

## Why this is different

Traditional delivery often breaks traceability between analysis and implementation.

BIMpact preserves continuity through:

- explicit decision records
- typed artefacts and relations
- append-only governance history
- reusable context for humans and AI runtimes

---

## Founder-origin logic

The method is grounded in a practical transfer of BIM discipline to AI-era delivery.

Original BIM principles applied to context and automation:

- a central model instead of disconnected documents
- collaborative and role-aware contributions
- linked entities instead of isolated notes
- data-driven coordination instead of manual reconciliation

This is the shift from building custom one-off tools to operating a system that can repeatedly generate reliable tools from maintained context.
