# BIMpact.io - Domains

## Why domains exist

BIMpact has one core identity: Context Information Model + Intent Management System.

Domains exist to show where this method is currently applied with different depth levels.

---

## Domain map

### BIM

Maturity: active

Deep operational experience from architect-to-BIM-management practice and domain validation through UC001.

Route: `/domains/bim`

### BI and analytics

Maturity: emerging

Power BI and Excel-centered delivery patterns for operational insight, governance metrics, and decision support.

Route: `/domains/bi`

### Microsoft ecosystem

Maturity: emerging

Power Platform workflows, custom agents, and orchestration patterns across Microsoft collaboration and automation surfaces.

Route: `/domains/microsoft-ecosystem`

### Public administration

Maturity: emerging

Method adaptation for public-sector constraints: governance, compliance, auditability, and multi-stakeholder process complexity.

Route: `/domains/public-administration`

### Maker Lab (Arduino / Raspberry)

Maturity: exploratory

AI-assisted physical prototyping concepts where product descriptions can be transformed into parts lists, cost approximations, and starter code.

Route: `/domains/maker-lab`
